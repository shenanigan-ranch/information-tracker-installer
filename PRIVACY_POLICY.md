# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Shenanigan Ranch Information Tracker Installer ("the Installer").

## Information Collection
- The Installer does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
- No user data is sent to external servers or third parties.

## Usage Data
- The Installer may generate local logs for troubleshooting purposes. These logs are stored only on your device and are not shared.

## Third-Party Services
- The Installer does not integrate with or use any third-party services that collect user data.

## Security
- The Installer is designed to operate locally and does not require an internet connection.
- All files and data remain on your device.

## Changes to This Policy
- Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be updated in this file and reflected with a new effective date.

## Contact
For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer or refer to the project repository.
